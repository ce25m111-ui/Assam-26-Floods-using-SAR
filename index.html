Radar Scattering Mechanism

Sentinel-1 (C-band, 5.6 cm) identifies open-water inundation through specular reflection reduction, whereas NISAR (L-band, 24 cm) penetrates emergent vegetation and submerged paddy canopies via double-bounce scattering.

Orbital Swath & Track Notes

Assam is covered using 2 orbital tracks of Sentinel-1 and 2 orbital tracks of NISAR during peak flooding (19–21 July 2026). Districts within overlapping swaths provide dual-sensor maps; single-swath districts display their respective sensor.
